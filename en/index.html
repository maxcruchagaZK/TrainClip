Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of videos, their length and the format. Tell us what you need and we will send you a quote.

Do the videos work with Buk, Talana or Asana?

Yes. We deliver the videos as MP4 files and your team uploads them to the platform it already uses. You don’t need to install anything or connect any systems.

Do we have to use avatars?

No. We can film your people, use avatars or combine both.

Who approves the content?

Your company. No video is delivered as final without review by the person responsible for the process.

What about my employees’ image and voice?

We only create an avatar with the person’s written consent. We agree which videos use it, for how long, and what happens if they leave the company.

Do the safety videos meet mandatory training requirements?

The videos support your training, but they don’t replace your company’s legal obligations. We work with content provided and approved by your safety team.

Where do you film?

We are based in Santiago, Chile. For other cities, travel and accommodation are quoted separately.
